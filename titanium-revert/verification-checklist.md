## Open questions for interviews and calls

1. What do Perryman, Howmet, TIMET and IperionX pay for certified domestic 6-4 turnings, and what oxygen, nitrogen, iron and tungsten limits do they set?
2. What does 6-4 slab or ingot sell for today? Pull the Fastmarkets MB-TI-0004 index and ask a plate buyer.
3. How much titanium scrap do Memphis implant makers generate, who buys it now, and on what contract?
4. Is Boeing's clause D204 still in force, and which Southeast shops machine Boeing-furnished titanium?
5. How does DLA Disposition Services choose who receives depot titanium scrap?
6. Will DIBC run another titanium round, and what does membership through Advanced Technology International cost?
7. Did Perryman finish its expansion to 42M lb a year? Is American Titanium Metal's North Carolina campus under construction?
8. What would a 3,500 t a year plasma hearth plus VAR cost in 2027 dollars? Ask Retech, ALD and Consarc for budgetary quotes.
9. Which candidate sites in Tennessee, western Pennsylvania and eastern Ohio can supply a multi-megawatt furnace load, and at what rate?
10. What do NFPA 484 and the local fire marshal require for chip storage at the melt site?
11. What does TIMET charge to toll-melt a trial heat, and how long is the wait?
12. Which plate mills will roll outside slab, and on what terms?
13. How many tons of titanium scrap did the US export in 2025, by country and grade? Pull HTS 8108.30 from USA Trade Online.
14. What would a 1,000–2,000 t a year turnings cleaning line cost, and what does a processor charge to toll-clean turnings?
15. How much working capital does a 3,500 t plant carry? The scrap study estimates $20–30M, which the capex range leaves out.
