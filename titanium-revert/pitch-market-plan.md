## The pitch

**Revert Metals turns American titanium scrap into defense-grade titanium slab in Tennessee, adding domestic metal without a new sponge plant.**

**Problem.** The US made no titanium sponge in 2025 and imported a record 44,000 t, 73% of it from Japan ([USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-titanium.pdf)). Titanium in defense aircraft, missiles, ships and vehicles must be melted in the US or a qualifying country ([DFARS 252.225-7009](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/48/252.225-7009)). ATI quoted about 20 months for premium titanium in August 2026, against a record $4.4B backlog ([Q2 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/ati-ati-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)).

**Insight.** Machining a forged titanium part throws away about 90% of the metal, at a 9:1 buy-to-fly ratio ([White Rose study](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/158586/1/metals-10-00296.pdf)). Scrap is 30 to 50% of TIMET's melt input ([TIMET](https://timet.com/capabilities/global-manufacturing/scrap-recycling.html)), and 32,000 t was imported in 2025. A cold hearth furnace can melt 100% scrap and trap the tool-bit fragments that keep chips out of VAR-only melts, so domestic scrap can become domestic metal without new sponge.

**Solution.** Two businesses in sequence.

1. **Revert supply program (2027 to 2029).** Collect, segregate and certify titanium scrap from Memphis implant makers, Southeast defense shops, new defense factories and military depots. Sell certified lots to Perryman, Howmet, TIMET and IperionX.
2. **Tennessee melt shop (from 2030).** A plasma or electron beam cold hearth plus VAR, sized at 3,000 to 4,000 t a year. It casts slab for armor and naval plate first and adds aerospace ingot after qualification.

**Why now.**

- **Government scrap.** In January 2026 the government transferred 290 t of titanium scrap to IperionX at no cost and paid out the last of a $47.1M IBAS award ([IperionX](https://iperionx.com/2026/01/16/iperionx-receives-final-4-6m-in-dow-funding-and-290-metric-tons-of-titanium-scrap-from-u-s-government/)).
- **New DoD money.** The Defense Industrial Base Consortium's August 2026 call asks for domestic titanium projects, "including sponge and sponge substitutes" ([BWCO summary](https://www.bwcoconsulting.com/fod/dibc-critical-minerals-rpp-cm-26-02)).
- **Cheaper debt.** The 2025 reconciliation act gave the Office of Strategic Capital $500M of credit subsidy, enough for up to about $100B of loans for critical-minerals and related projects ([DoW](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4270722/office-of-strategic-capital-announces-first-loan-through-dod-agreement-with-mp/)).
- **Naval demand.** The Navy awarded about $76.6B for 14 submarines on July 30, 2026 ([Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/branches/navy/2026-07-30/navy-contracts-14-new-submarines-22411844.html)).
- **Proof abroad.** France's EcoTitanium has made recycled aerospace ingot since 2017 on a EUR 48M plant ([EIB](https://www.eib.org/en/stories/titanium-recycling)).

**Market.** US melters use roughly $200M to $400M of titanium scrap a year, and US mill products are worth roughly $2B to $7B. The derivation is in the market section.

**Business model.**

- **Phase 1** earns about $0.25 to $0.90/lb on certified lots. Every lot is bought against a melter order, so the company never holds unsold metal.
- **Phase 2** earns the conversion margin from roughly $3/lb scrap to slab and ingot priced off the [Fastmarkets 6-4 ingot index](https://www.fastmarkets.com/commodity-prices/MB-TI-0004). It also sells toll melting to shops and primes that want a closed loop.

**Competition.**

- **Processors** (Aperam Recycling, PCC Revert, CRONIMET, IMET, Goldman, Mega Metals) sell feed and do not melt.
- **Incumbent melters** (TIMET, ATI, Howmet, Perryman) are full and serve aerospace contracts first.
- **IperionX** makes powder and parts, not slab, and is also a scrap buyer.
- **American Titanium Metal** announced an $868M melt-and-roll campus in Fayetteville, NC in 2024. It is the one to watch, and its 2026 status is unconfirmed ([NC Commerce](https://commerce.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2024/07/23/manufacturing-company-announces-north-carolina-investment-8678-million-cumberland-county)).
- **No US company** has built a scrap-first titanium melter.

**Moat.** Locked scrap supply with custody records behind every lot, domestic-melt status, DoD program qualification, and TVA power at about 6.7 cents/kWh against a US industrial average of 8.6 cents ([EIA via Missouri Partnership](https://missouripartnership.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Electricity-Price-Data-4.pdf)).

**Team.** The founder is CEO and owns capital, government programs and supply contracts. The company needs two more people before Phase 2: a titanium melt metallurgist as cofounder and an operations lead from a specialty scrap yard.

**The ask.** A $1.0M to $1.5M pre-seed on a post-money SAFE, for 12 months of the supply program. It pays for two hires, an analyzer lab, insurance, inventory float, and DIBC and SBIR proposals. The milestones it buys:

- 10 signed shop supply agreements
- 2 melters buying repeat lots
- 200 t shipped
- a metallurgist cofounder signed
- a Phase 2 concept ready for the next DIBC round

## Market analysis

US melters used an estimated 32,000 to 52,000 t of titanium scrap in 2025, worth about $200M to $400M. Of that, 32,000 t was imported, and that is the tonnage a domestic revert supply replaces.

### Market size

| Layer | Size | How it is derived |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US scrap fed to melters (Phase 1 TAM) | 32,000 to 52,000 t; $200M to $400M a year | Imports of 32,000 t set the floor ([USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-titanium.pdf)). If scrap is 42 to 54% of melt input, as USGS's long-run 54% figure suggests ([USGS Circular 1196-Y](https://pubs.usgs.gov/circ/circ1196-Y)), total use reaches 52,000 t. Valued at $2.45 to $3.49/lb |

- **Commercial aircraft.** Boeing built 8 787s a month in January 2026 and aims for 10 during 2026; titanium is about 15% of a 787's structural weight ([SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/01/27/boeing-looks-to-build-on-momentum-at-sc-dreamliner-plant/), [MFG Robots](https://www.mfgrobots.com/Article/material/metal/194.html)).

- **Submarines.** The Navy awarded about $76.6B for 14 boats in July 2026, and Anduril and the Navy are putting $6.6B into submarine parts production ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/techwatch/2026/10/06/anduril-us-navy-invest-66-billion-to-boost-submarine-parts-production/)).
- **Missiles and space.** Castelion raised $1B in August 2026 under a framework for at least 500 missiles a year ([NM News](https://nm.news/2026/09/03/castelion-raises-1-billion/)); Blue Origin's Huntsville plant can build up to 42 engines a year ([Made in Alabama](https://www.madeinalabama.com/2020/02/blue-origin-officially-opens-alabama-rocket-engine-factory)).
- **Implants.** Memphis-area medical device companies number 47 and employ more than 16,800 people ([EDGE Memphis](https://edgemem.com/key-industry-life-sciences/)).

### Supply constraints

- **Sponge.** China made about 70% of world sponge in 2025. US imports come from Japan, Kazakhstan and Saudi Arabia ([USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-titanium.pdf)).
- **Melt.** ATI quotes about 20 months for premium titanium. It holds new projects to a 30% return hurdle and asks customers to fund new assets, so incumbents will not build ahead of demand ([Q2 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/ati-ati-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)).

### Price risk

Argus 6-4 bulk weldables fell from $5.00 to $5.50/lb in October 2022 to $2.50 to $2.90/lb in January 2026 ([Argus 2022](<https://view.argusmedia.com/rs/584-BUW-606/images/Argus%20Metal%20Prices%20(2022-07-01).pdf>)). USGS's imported scrap value fell from $8.50/kg in 2024 to $7.70/kg in 2025. A revert business that holds inventory loses money when prices fall, so Phase 1 buys only against melter orders.

## Unit economics

In the base case Phase 1 earns $0.42 a pound and breaks even near 1,200 t a year. Phase 2 earns about $41M of gross margin at 3,500 t a year if slab sells near $12 a pound, and that slab price is the first number to validate. Lines marked as assumptions are placeholders for the buyer and shop interviews to replace.

### Step by step, from pitch to expansion

1. **Write the one-page pitch and a 10-slide deck** from this document, and test it on five people who work in metals.
2. **Run 20 shop interviews.** Ask about titanium volume by alloy and form, current buyer, price, contract terms (including Boeing's clause D204) and willingness to segregate.
3. **Run 4 melter interviews**, with Perryman, Howmet, TIMET and IperionX. Ask about lot specs, oxygen, nitrogen and iron limits, supplier approval steps, the price paid for certified domestic lots, and whether they would sign a letter of intent.
4. **Recruit the metallurgist cofounder** through TMS 2027, MIT materials science, Oak Ridge and former TIMET Henderson staff. Agree vesting before any equity is promised.
5. **Incorporate** a Delaware C-corp. File 83(b) elections within 30 days, put four-year founder vesting in place, and run a trademark search on the name.
6. **Win the first dollar** from the President's Innovation Challenge, an Ingenuity Award, GC Venture Fellows or Z Fellows.
7. **Pass Gate A.** Hold letters of intent for 300+ t a year of supply and one melter letter at a price that clears cost.
8. **Raise the pre-seed** of $1.0M to $1.5M on a post-money SAFE.
9. **Ship the first lot** through a toll processor, against a melter purchase order, with a full chemistry and custody packet.
10. **Register for government work.** Get a SAM.gov registration and CAGE code, join the Defense Industrial Base Consortium, and file an SBIR Phase I.
11. **Pass Gate B.** Ship 200 t to 2 repeat buyers at $0.30/lb or better.
12. **Raise the seed and a credit line.** Open the Tennessee yard, earn ISO 9001 and scale to 1,000 to 1,500 t a year.
13. **Commission the melt engineering study.** Pick the site, settle on PAM or EB, and size the furnace with a vendor such as Retech, ALD or Consarc.
14. **Submit the melt shop to DoD.** File with DIBC or IBAS and open OSC and EXIM loan talks. Pass Gate C with an award or term sheet plus a slab offtake letter.
15. **Build, cast and qualify.** Build the melt shop, cast the first slab, qualify armor plate, and pass Gate D on the first defense order.
16. **Expand.** Qualify aerospace ingot, add a second hearth and buy small revert processors.
