# Revert Metals — Pitch, Market Analysis and Plan

2026-10-14 · @Hunter

## Summary and corrections

In 2025, US titanium melters ran on 44,000 t of imported sponge, the raw metal that mills melt, and 32,000 t of imported scrap, and the US made no sponge of its own ([USGS MCS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-titanium.pdf)). Revert Metals (a working name) turns US scrap into a domestic feed that displaces imported scrap and sponge, by melting it on a cold hearth, a furnace that can run on pure scrap, in a new US melt shop. The company secures scrap by contract through existing processors, proves its slab with trial melts at an existing furnace, and then builds its own plant at a site chosen by a 2028 study.

That is a shift from the October 13 thesis. Checking its claims showed that the scrap-processing wedge is more crowded and has a thinner margin than it assumed, while the melt opportunity is better funded and has a clearer precedent. The table lists what changed. This version also skips the scrap-yard business to go straight to melting, and it leaves the site to a study instead of committing to Tennessee.

## The pitch

**Revert Metals turns American titanium scrap into defense-grade titanium slab in a new US melt shop, adding domestic metal without a new sponge plant.**

**Problem.** The US made no titanium sponge in 2025 and imported a record 44,000 t; Japan supplied 73% of imports from January to July ([USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-titanium.pdf)). Titanium in defense aircraft, missiles, ships and vehicles must be melted in the US or a qualifying country ([DFARS 252.225-7009](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/48/252.225-7009)). ATI quoted about 20 months for premium titanium in August 2026, against a record $4.4B backlog ([Q2 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/ati-ati-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)).

**Insight.** Machining a forged titanium part throws away about 90% of the metal, at a 9:1 buy-to-fly ratio ([White Rose study](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/158586/1/metals-10-00296.pdf)). Scrap is 30 to 50% of TIMET's melt input ([TIMET](https://timet.com/capabilities/global-manufacturing/scrap-recycling.html)), and 32,000 t was imported in 2025. A cold hearth furnace can melt 100% scrap and trap the tool-bit fragments that keep chips out of VAR-only melts, so domestic scrap can become domestic metal without new sponge.

**Solution.** Go straight to melting, in three steps.

1.  **Secure the scrap (2027 to 2028).** Sign multi-year supply agreements with Memphis implant makers, defense shops in the Southeast and Midwest, new defense factories and military depots. Existing processors such as Goldman Titanium clean and certify it under contract, so the company never runs a scrap yard.

2.  **Prove the metal (2027 to 2028).** Pay an existing furnace to melt trial batches from that scrap; TIMET sells this as toll melting. Test the slab, and get armor and naval plate buyers and a rolling mill to sign letters of intent before building anything.

3.  **Build the melt shop (from 2030).** A plasma or electron beam cold hearth plus VAR, sized at 3,000 to 4,000 t a year, at a site chosen by a 2028 site study. The hearth casts slab for armor and naval plate first; the VAR remelts ingot for aerospace after qualification.

**Why now.**

- **Government scrap.** In January 2026 the government transferred 290 t of titanium scrap to IperionX at no cost and paid out the last of a $47.1M IBAS award ([IperionX](https://iperionx.com/2026/01/16/iperionx-receives-final-4-6m-in-dow-funding-and-290-metric-tons-of-titanium-scrap-from-u-s-government/)).

- **New DoD money.** The Defense Industrial Base Consortium's August 2026 call asks for domestic titanium projects, "including sponge and sponge substitutes" ([BWCO summary](https://www.bwcoconsulting.com/fod/dibc-critical-minerals-rpp-cm-26-02)).

- **Cheaper debt.** The 2025 reconciliation act gave the Office of Strategic Capital $500M of credit subsidy, enough for up to about $100B of loans for critical-minerals and related projects ([DoW](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4270722/office-of-strategic-capital-announces-first-loan-through-dod-agreement-with-mp/)).

- **Naval demand.** The Navy awarded about $76.6B for 14 submarines on July 30, 2026 ([Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/branches/navy/2026-07-30/navy-contracts-14-new-submarines-22411844.html)).

- **Proof abroad.** France's EcoTitanium has melted aerospace scrap into ingot since 2017 on a EUR 48M plant ([EIB](https://www.eib.org/en/stories/titanium-recycling)).

**Market.** US melters use roughly $170M to $400M of titanium scrap a year, and US mill products are worth roughly $2B to $7B. The derivation is in the market section.

**Business model.**

- **Before the plant**, revenue is small: certified scrap or toll-melted slab sold back-to-back against signed orders. That stage exists to prove price, product and supply.

- **The melt shop** earns the conversion margin from roughly $3/lb scrap to slab and ingot priced off the [Fastmarkets 6-4 ingot index](https://www.fastmarkets.com/commodity-prices/MB-TI-0004). It also sells toll melting to shops and primes that want a closed loop.

**Competition.**

- **Processors** (Aperam Recycling, PCC Revert, CRONIMET, IMET, Goldman, Mega Metals) sell feed and do not melt.

- **Incumbent melters** (TIMET, ATI, Howmet, Perryman) are short of qualified premium capacity and serve aerospace contracts first.

- **IperionX** makes powder and parts, not slab, and is also a scrap buyer.

- **American Titanium Metal** announced an $868M melt-and-roll campus in Fayetteville, NC in 2024. Its 2026 status is unconfirmed ([NC Commerce](https://commerce.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2024/07/23/manufacturing-company-announces-north-carolina-investment-8678-million-cumberland-county)).

- **No US company** sells hearth-melted slab from domestic scrap as its main business; the scrap-heavy hearths at TIMET and Perryman feed their owners' own mills.

**Moat.** Multi-year scrap supply contracts with custody records behind every lot, qualified slab with named defense buyers, and DoD program backing. None of these depends on which state the plant sits in.

**Team.** The founder is CEO and owns capital, government programs and supply contracts. The company needs a titanium melt metallurgist as cofounder before the pre-seed, and soon after an operations lead who has run scrap processing or melt-shop logistics.

**The ask.** A $1.0M to $1.5M pre-seed on a post-money SAFE, for 12 to 18 months of proving the metal. It pays for the metallurgist cofounder and one operations hire, trial melts and slab tests, the engineering and site study, and DIBC and SBIR proposals. The milestones it buys:

- a metallurgist cofounder signed

- scrap supply agreements covering at least 2,000 t a year

- trial slab melted at an existing furnace and tested to spec

- letters of intent from a plate buyer and a rolling mill

- a site chosen and a melt shop proposal ready for the next DIBC round

## Market analysis

US melters used an estimated 32,000 to 52,000 t of titanium scrap in 2025, worth about $170M to $400M. Of that, 32,000 t was imported, and that is the tonnage a domestic revert supply replaces.

### Demand drivers

- **Commercial aircraft.** Boeing built 8 787s a month in January 2026 and aims for 10 during 2026; titanium is about 15% of a 787's structural weight ([SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/01/27/boeing-looks-to-build-on-momentum-at-sc-dreamliner-plant/), [MFG Robots](https://www.mfgrobots.com/Article/material/metal/194.html)).

- **Fighters.** Lockheed delivered a record 191 F-35s in 2025 and says it can sustain 156 a year ([AeroCorner](https://aerocorner.com/news/f-35-production-holds-156-aircraft/)).

- **Submarines.** The Navy awarded about $76.6B for 14 boats in July 2026, and Anduril and the Navy are putting $6.6B into submarine parts production ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/techwatch/2026/10/06/anduril-us-navy-invest-66-billion-to-boost-submarine-parts-production/)).

- **Missiles and space.** Castelion raised $1B in August 2026 under a framework for at least 500 missiles a year ([NM News](https://nm.news/2026/09/03/castelion-raises-1-billion/)); Blue Origin's Huntsville plant can build up to 42 engines a year ([Made in Alabama](https://www.madeinalabama.com/2020/02/blue-origin-officially-opens-alabama-rocket-engine-factory)).

- **Implants.** Memphis-area medical device companies number 47 and employ more than 16,800 people ([EDGE Memphis](https://edgemem.com/key-industry-life-sciences/)).

### Supply constraints

- **Sponge.** China made about 70% of world sponge in 2025. US imports come from Japan, Kazakhstan and Saudi Arabia ([USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-titanium.pdf)).

- **Melt.** ATI quotes about 20 months for premium titanium. It holds new projects to a 30% return hurdle and asks customers to fund new assets, so incumbents will not build ahead of demand ([Q2 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/13/ati-ati-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)).

### Price risk

Argus 6-4 bulk weldables fell from $5.00 to $5.50/lb in a 2022 sample to $2.50 to $2.90/lb in January 2026 ([Argus 2022](https://view.argusmedia.com/rs/584-BUW-606/images/Argus%20Metal%20Prices%20(2022-07-01).pdf)). USGS's imported scrap value fell from $8.50/kg in 2024 to $7.70/kg in 2025. A revert business that holds inventory loses money when prices fall, so the company buys scrap only against signed orders or scheduled melts.

| Slab and ingot price | $10 to $14/lb, $77M to $108M revenue                       | Assumption; replace with the [Fastmarkets 6-4 ingot index](https://www.fastmarkets.com/commodity-prices/MB-TI-0004)                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Gross margin         | $20M to $63M; base $41M at $12/lb and $3.25/lb conversion | Revenue minus feed and conversion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

Each $1/lb of slab price moves gross margin by $7.7M. Power price moves it far less: if the plant uses under 35 GWh a year (assumption), a 2-cent gap in power price between states changes margin by under $0.7M. Industrial rates run from about 6.7 cents/kWh in Tennessee to an 8.6-cent national average ([EIA via Missouri Partnership](https://missouripartnership.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Electricity-Price-Data-4.pdf)), so the site study weighs power availability, melt workforce and rolling access above the rate.

Qualification and furnace delivery set the ramp. Perryman's furnaces took over two years from the late-2017 order to 2020 commissioning ([New Equipment Digest](https://www.newequipment.com/metal-forming/article/21121603/perryman-commissions-furnaces-retech-systems-llc)), so full output lands around 2033. Expect 12 to 18 months for each aerospace customer, and ballistic testing to MIL-DTL-46077 before armor plate sells.

## Roadmap

Four go or no-go gates, A to D, separate the phases drawn below. The first dollar is a student prize or a small check in spring 2027, the first institutional money is a $1.0M to $1.5M pre-seed after Gate A, and the melt shop is financed only after a DoD award or loan term sheet at Gate C.

### Step by step, from pitch to expansion

1.  **Write the one-page pitch and a 10-slide deck** from this document, and test it on five people who work in metals.

2.  **Recruit the metallurgist cofounder** through QLab, TMS 2027, MIT materials science, Oak Ridge and former TIMET, ATI and Perryman staff. Agree vesting before any equity is promised.

3.  **Get three numbers.** A slab price from plate buyers and the Fastmarkets ingot index, budgetary furnace quotes from Retech, ALD and Consarc, and a toll-melt quote from TIMET.

4.  **Run 20 scrap-source calls** in the Memphis, Huntsville and Midwest clusters on volume, alloy, current buyer and contract terms, including Boeing's clause D204.

5.  **Run partner calls.** Goldman and IMET on toll processing, a plate mill on rolling outside slab, and armor and naval plate buyers on spec, volume and price.

6.  **Incorporate** a Delaware C-corp once the cofounder agrees. File 83(b) elections within 30 days, put four-year founder vesting in place, and run a trademark search on the name.

7.  **Win the first dollar** from the President's Innovation Challenge, an Ingenuity Award, GC Venture Fellows or Z Fellows.

8.  **Pass Gate A.** Cofounder signed, numbers that work, and one buyer and one scrap source in writing.

9.  **Raise the pre-seed** of $1.0M to $1.5M on a post-money SAFE.

10. **Register for government work.** Get a SAM.gov registration and CAGE code, join the Defense Industrial Base Consortium, and file an SBIR Phase I.

11. **Prove the metal.** Sign scrap supply through processors, toll-melt the first trial heats, and test the slab to the armor spec.

12. **Run the site study.** Weigh melt workforce, rolling access, power availability and incentives across Tennessee, western Pennsylvania and eastern Ohio.

13. **Pass Gate B.** Trial slab in spec, 2,000+ t a year of scrap signed, and letters of intent from a plate buyer and a rolling mill.

14. **Finance the plant.** Raise the seed, submit the melt shop to DIBC or IBAS, and open OSC and EXIM loan talks. Pass Gate C with an award or term sheet plus a slab offtake letter.

15. **Build, cast and qualify.** Order the furnaces, build at the chosen site, cast the first slab, qualify armor plate, and pass Gate D on the first defense order.

16. **Expand.** Qualify aerospace ingot, add a second hearth, and add owned scrap yards or buy small processors.
