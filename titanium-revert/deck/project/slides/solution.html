The solution

Collect US titanium scrap now, melt it in Tennessee from 2030

Phase 1 · 2027 to 2029

Revert supply program

  • Segregate, certify and trace scrap from implant makers, defense shops, new factories and depots
  • Sell certified lots to Perryman, Howmet, TIMET and IperionX
  • Earns $0.25 to $0.90/lb and builds the supply base

Phase 2 · from 2030

Tennessee melt shop

  • Plasma or electron beam cold hearth plus VAR, 3,000 to 4,000 t a year
  • Slab for armor and naval plate first, aerospace ingot after qualification
  • Domestic melt under DFARS, on TVA power

Phase 2 starts only after a DoD award or loan term sheet and a slab offtake letter

Phase 1 is a supply program, not the profit engine. It earns a thin spread but locks in the scrap, the shop relationships, the quality system and the custody data a melt shop needs on day one. Phase 2 is where the value is: melting scrap into slab and ingot that sells far above scrap prices.
