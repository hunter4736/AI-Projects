The solution

Secure the scrap, prove the metal, then build the melt shop

Step 1 · 2027 to 2028

Secure the scrap

  • Multi-year supply from implant makers, defense shops and depots
  • Processors clean and certify it under contract

Step 2 · 2027 to 2028

Prove the metal

  • Trial melts at an existing furnace
  • Slab tested; a buyer and a rolling mill sign letters of intent

Step 3 · from 2030

Build the melt shop

  • Cold hearth plus VAR, 3,000 to 4,000 t a year
  • Site chosen by a 2028 site study

The plant is built only after a DoD award or loan term sheet and a slab offtake letter

The company does not run a scrap yard. Processors like Goldman Titanium already clean and certify scrap for a fee, so we contract supply through them and put our capital into the melt, where the margin is. TIMET sells toll melting, so trial batches prove the slab before we own a furnace. The site study weighs melt workforce, rolling access, power availability and incentives; Tennessee and western Pennsylvania or eastern Ohio are the lead candidates.
