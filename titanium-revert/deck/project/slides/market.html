The scrap market floor is the 32,000 t the US imported in 2025 at about $7.70 per kilogram. If scrap is 42 to 54 percent of melt input, as USGS's long-run figure suggests, total use reaches about 52,000 t. Mill products come from melt input times a 0.6 to 0.8 yield times 20 to 40 dollars a pound. These are derived; buyer interviews replace them first.
