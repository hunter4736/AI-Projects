Processors are partners as much as competitors: Goldman sells toll processing, and IperionX buys scrap. TIMET can melt our trial batches and plate mills can roll our slab, so the incumbents are partners now and competitors later, but they hold new projects to a 30 percent return hurdle and ask customers to fund new furnaces, so they are not racing to build scrap-first capacity.
