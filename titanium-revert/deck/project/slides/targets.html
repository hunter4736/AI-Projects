Go to market

Scrap comes in under contract; slab goes to rolling mills and defense buyers

Scrap sources

  • Memphis implant makers, 47 firms
  • Huntsville engine and defense shops
  • Anduril Arsenal-1, Columbus, OH
  • Military depots through DLA
  • Warsaw, IN implant contract makers

Partners and buyers

  • Goldman and IMET certify scrap
  • TIMET melts the trial batches
  • Plate mills roll the slab
  • Armor and naval plate buyers

Sources: EDGE Memphis; Made in Alabama; IndustryWeek; DLA; company sites and filings

Memphis is the wedge for supply: about 47 medical device companies and more than 16,800 employees, machining mostly 6-4 ELI and CP titanium that sits outside Boeing's closed loop. Anduril's Arsenal-1 is new, so it has no legacy scrap contracts, and the government gave IperionX 290 t of depot scrap, so DLA is a channel to pursue. On the partner side, Goldman Titanium sells toll processing, TIMET sells toll melting, plate mills such as Perryman and ATI can roll outside slab, and the Army and the Department of War already buy ballistic titanium plate. Howmet, IperionX and ferrotitanium makers are fallback outlets for scrap we do not melt.
