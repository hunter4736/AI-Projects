Go to market

First scrap from within 400 miles of Nashville; first buyers already melt revert

Scrap sources

  • Memphis implant makers, 47 firms
  • Huntsville engine and defense shops
  • Anduril Arsenal-1, Columbus, OH
  • Military depots through DLA
  • Warsaw, IN implant contract makers

Buyers

  • Perryman, buys on the open market
  • Howmet, whose melt uses revert
  • TIMET, with 30–50% scrap input
  • IperionX, scaling to 1,400 t a year

Sources: EDGE Memphis; Made in Alabama; IndustryWeek; DLA; company sites and filings

Memphis is the wedge: about 47 medical device companies and more than 16,800 employees, machining clean single-alloy 6-4 ELI that sits outside Boeing's closed loop. Huntsville is about 110 miles from Nashville and Memphis about 210. Anduril's Arsenal-1 is new, so it has no legacy scrap contracts. The government gave IperionX 290 t of scrap, so depot scrap is a channel to pursue. On the buy side, Perryman owns a revert processor and buys on the open market, Howmet's Canton melt uses revert, TIMET runs 30 to 50 percent scrap, and IperionX wants high-oxygen turnings that melters discount.
