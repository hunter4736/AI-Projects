Roadmap

Four gates stand between today and a melt shop

PhaseWhenWhat happensGate to pass next
Validate (now)Oct 2026 to Jun 2027Recruit a metallurgist cofounder; get slab, furnace and toll-melt prices; win first prize moneyA: cofounder signed, numbers that work, one buyer and one scrap source in writing
Prove the metalJul 2027 to Dec 2028$1.0M to $1.5M pre-seed; scrap supply through processors; trial melts; site studyB: trial slab in spec, 2,000+ t a year of scrap signed, plate buyer and mill LOIs
Finance2029$4M to $8M seed; DIBC or IBAS proposal; OSC and EXIM loan talks; site chosenC: DoD award or loan term sheet, plus a slab offtake letter
Build2030 to 2032$55M to $75M from DoD cost share, debt and a Series A; first slabD: armor plate qualified and a first defense order
Expand2033 onwardAerospace qualification, a second hearth, toll melting, owned scrap supplyOngoing

A failed gate means a pivot instead of a bigger raise, such as sourcing scrap for IperionX or a melter, or licensing the supply contracts and trial data

The first dollar is a student prize or a small check in spring 2027. The first institutional money is the pre-seed after Gate A, and it pays for proving the metal, not for a scrap yard. The melt shop is financed only after Gate C, with a DoD cost share of 30 to 60 percent, a loan from the Office of Strategic Capital or EXIM, a Series A, and incentives from whichever state wins the site study. IperionX followed a similar path: a small DPA award, then a $47.1M IBAS award.
