Titanium scrap the US imported in 2025

A cold hearth furnace melts all-scrap charges and traps the tool-bit fragments a vacuum arc melt cannot remove. US scrap can become US metal without a sponge plant.

Sources: White Rose Research Online (buy-to-fly); TIMET scrap recycling page; USGS MCS 2026

Scrap is already half the story in titanium. The point is that a hearth melter does not need sponge at all if it has enough clean, certified scrap. The constraint is getting that scrap segregated and certified, and owning the furnace.
