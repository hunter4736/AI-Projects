Business model

Phase 1 earns $0.42 a pound; Phase 2 earns about $41M a year

Phase 1, per pound of 6-4 turnings

Sell to the melter$3.10
Buy at the shop dock$2.20
Freight, processing, lab$0.48
Gross margin$0.42

Breakeven near 1,200 t a year. The low case loses $0.18/lb, so a lot is bought only when the melter's signed price clears cost.

Phase 2, 3,500 t a year, $M

Revenue at $12/lb92
Scrap feed at $3.10/lb26
Conversion at $3.25/lb25
Gross margin41

Capex $55M to $75M. Each $1/lb of slab price moves gross margin by $7.7M.

Base cases. Buy price, processing cost, conversion cost and slab price are assumptions that buyer interviews will replace.

Phase 1 margins are thin by design; it exists to secure supply. Phase 2 economics hinge on the slab and ingot price, which we will benchmark against the Fastmarkets 6-4 ingot index and confirm in melter and plate-buyer interviews. Capex comes from EcoTitanium (EUR 48M for up to 4,000 t a year in 2017) and ATI Richland (about $40M for a second EB furnace plus VAR), adjusted for US construction.
