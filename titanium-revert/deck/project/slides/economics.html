Base cases. Trial costs, conversion cost and slab price are assumptions that quotes and buyer interviews will replace.

The early stage spends rather than earns. Trial melts at an existing furnace, slab testing and a site study cost roughly $310K to $720K and prove the product before any capex. The melt shop economics hinge on the slab and ingot price, benchmarked against the Fastmarkets 6-4 ingot index. Sourcing feed directly at about $2.68 a pound instead of the $3.10 market price would add roughly $3.5M a year, which is what the supply contracts are worth. Capex comes from EcoTitanium (EUR 48M for up to 4,000 t a year in 2017) and ATI Richland (about $40M for a second EB furnace plus VAR).
