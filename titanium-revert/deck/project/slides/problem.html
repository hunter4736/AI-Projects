Sources: USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026; DFARS 252.225-7009; ATI Q2 2026 earnings call

The US closed its last sponge plants: Henderson was idled in 2020, Rowley in 2016, and a small Utah unit in 2024. Every US melter now runs on imported sponge, mostly Japanese. Defense rules require titanium in covered items to be melted in the US or a qualifying country, so demand piles onto a few qualified furnaces. ATI told investors in August 2026 that premium titanium runs about 20 months.
