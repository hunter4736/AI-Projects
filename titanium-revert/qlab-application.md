## What problem do you want to solve, and for whom?

Every US fighter jet, submarine and missile depends on a metal America can no longer make. In 2025 the US produced zero titanium sponge, the raw metal, and imported a record 44,000 tons, 73% from Japan. China makes 70% of the world's supply. A Pacific crisis could cut off most new titanium. The Navy, its submarine yards (Electric Boat, HII Newport News) and the F-35 supply chain carry this risk, and premium titanium already takes about 20 months to get. We will melt America's own titanium scrap into new defense-grade slab, starting with armor and naval plate.

I came across a 62-week quote for American-melted titanium and wanted to know why a defense supplier would wait more than a year for an American metal.
